Academy Lesson

Understanding Sleep

Learn the fundamentals of sleep, sleep quality and daily recovery.

Section 01

What is sleep?

Sleep is a recurring period of rest during which the body and brain go through a number of biological processes. Sleep is not simply inactive time.

Agent perspectiveFor your wellness profile, we're interested in how you experience sleep and how it relates to your broader routine.
Section 02

Sleep quality

Sleep quality can involve more than the number of hours you spend asleep. Consistency, difficulty falling asleep, interruptions and how you feel after waking can all provide useful context.

What this means for youYour current profile identifies sleep quality as one of your concerns.
Section 03

Your daily routine

Sleep doesn't exist in isolation. Your daily schedule, activity, environment and habits can provide useful context when thinking about your wellness goals.

Agent next stepThe next part of your profile will collect additional routine information so the agent has more context.
Section 04

What should you track?

Before making a recommendation, the agent can help you establish what information is actually useful to track.
